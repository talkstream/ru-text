## What ru-text is

ru-text is a Russian text quality reference for AI coding agents. It consists of static
Markdown files that your agent reads locally. There is no server, no account, and no fee.

text: an explicit instruction from you overrides any of them. You are responsible for what

## What it does with your text

Nothing leaves your machine. ru-text collects, stores and transmits no data whatsoever —
see the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for the full statement.

ru-text reports findings and proposes replacements. It does not rewrite a file unless you
ask your agent to apply the changes, and quoted material, code and third-party text inside
your document are reproduced as they are.
